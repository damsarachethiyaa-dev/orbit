    ANSWERED.

    Does it work with every video?

    Almost every public one. Videos protected by DRM (Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video) and private videos that need a login can't be downloaded.

    Is it legal?

    Only download content you own, that is licensed for it, or that you have permission to save. You are responsible for how you use Orbit.

    What quality can I get?

    The best the source offers, or capped at 4K, 1440p, 1080p, 720p or 480p. Audio only exports as a 192 kbps MP3.

    Where do my files go?

    Orbit stores each file briefly so your browser can fetch it, then deletes it automatically after an hour.

    Why did a link fail?

    The video may be private, age restricted, region locked or removed. Sites also change often; Orbit updates its engine each time it starts.
