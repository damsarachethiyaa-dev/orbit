Is Orbit free?

Yes. Orbit is free, with no ads and no account.

Which sites work?

YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X, Facebook, Reddit, SoundCloud and over a thousand others. A few, such as Instagram and Vimeo, ask you to sign in first under Settings, then Site accounts.

Where do my downloads go?

Into your phone's own folders: Movies/Orbit, Pictures/Orbit and Music/Orbit. They show up in your gallery like any other video.

Why do I have to uninstall to update?

Only once, and only if you have version 1.0.22 to 1.0.27. Version 1.0.28 is signed with a different key, so Android can't update over the old app. Your saved videos stay in your gallery.

Is there a desktop version?

Yes, Orbit also runs on Windows. See Orbit for Windows.
